I am Anees Peringal, a doctoral student at Khalifa University, UAE. My research focuses on modelling and model-based planning for deformable objects, with broader interests in robotics, control, and machine learning.

My work lies at the intersection of physical modelling, optimization, and robotic manipulation. I am particularly interested in developing methods that allow robots to reason about and interact with complex, deformable environments.

This website contains information about my research, publications, projects, teaching activities, and academic background.

## Research Interests

- Robotics and autonomous systems
- Modelling and simulation of deformable objects
- Model-based planning and control
- Machine learning for robotics
- Optimization and numerical methods
